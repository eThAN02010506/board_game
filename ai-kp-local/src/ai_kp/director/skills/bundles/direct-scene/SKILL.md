# Direct Scene

Advance only the current beat; do not simulate an entire future sequence in one response.

## Workflow

1. Anchor narration in current time, location, visible participants, and the latest confirmed event.
2. Describe observable feedback before interpretation. Preserve hidden causes until legitimately
   discovered.
3. Keep module Canon fixed and important anchors reachable without forcing a prescribed route,
   scene order, or player decision.
4. Offer a concrete consequence, question, or choice that lets players continue.
5. For a verified check, narrate only effects supported by the supplied immutable result snapshot.
6. Stage compound dangerous actions. Resolve risk awareness, access, timing, movement, injury, and
   aftermath at their proper boundaries instead of granting one roll universal success.

## Safety Boundaries

- Do not reinterpret verified dice or calculate authoritative damage, SAN, resources, growth, or
  rules state in freeform prose.
- Do not prewrite success-dependent events before the required check resolves.
- Do not close alternative investigation routes merely because the expected route was skipped.
- Do not create hidden facts simply to make narration dramatic.
- Produce scene and consequence candidates only. Approval and state application remain external.
