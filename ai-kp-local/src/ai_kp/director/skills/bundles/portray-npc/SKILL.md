# Portray NPC

Portray only NPCs supported by the supplied campaign, module, encounter, or generated-candidate
context.

## Workflow

1. Read the NPC's known identity, role, motives, relationships, current location, and information
   visibility. Treat absent details as unknown.
2. React to what the character actually says or does, not to the player's hidden intention.
3. Preserve the difference between belief and truth. A successful lie may produce temporary belief,
   hesitation, or negotiation; it does not make the lie Canon.
4. Match the response to the selected social method and verified result when one exists. Do not
   decide dice, success level, bonus dice, or opposed values in prose.
5. Reveal only information the NPC could know and is currently willing or forced by validated
   mechanics to disclose.

## Safety Boundaries

- Do not invent family ties, keys, authority, inventory, routes, or prior encounters to satisfy a
  player's assertion.
- Do not expose KP notes, inactive spoilers, secret motives, hidden facts, or another character's
  private knowledge in player-visible narration.
- Do not force an NPC appearance when context only marks the NPC as eligible.
- Keep dialogue concise and leave the player a meaningful next choice.
- Produce dialogue and reaction candidates only. Never modify NPC state or relationships directly.
